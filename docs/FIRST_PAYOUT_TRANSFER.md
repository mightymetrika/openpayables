## One-shot policy

Migration 013 links an append-only attempt to the immutable pilot approval. The unique approval ID is the attempt's primary key; `openpayables-pilot-<approval UUID>` is its fixed Stripe idempotency key and transfer group. Execution requires the exact frozen recipient and integer cents, a separate operator execution approval reference, the dedicated LIVE DB/login, matching provider mode, and fresh evidence of the approved top-up's available balance transaction and net value, payer balance and recipient eligibility. The write credential must prove it belongs to that payer through the same preflight. The Accounts v2 recipient transfers capability must be active in addition to the existing v1 readiness checks. A successful preflight is consumed at most once and expires after 60 seconds. No caller-supplied amount or destination is passed to Stripe in place of the frozen instruction.

The attempt must be committed before one POST to Stripe. If the INSERT/commit acknowledgment fails, do not POST. If any later HTTP/transport/response/persistence failure occurs, the attempt remains permanently consumed. There are **no automatic or manual resend commands**, even if no transfer appears in the Dashboard or Stripe's idempotency retention window has elapsed. A crash before POST can therefore strand the pilot; this is intentional. Do not delete attempts, clone a LIVE database, or create replacement approvals to circumvent the policy. An operator must investigate unresolved outcomes using Stripe Workbench, the approval UUID, transfer group and idempotency key. The local ledger does not reserve funds at Stripe and cannot prevent external account activity. Keep one dedicated trusted operator installation and protect its database backups; a rollback to a pre-attempt backup is unsafe for execution.

## Reconciliation

Future CLI: `npm run live:transfer -- status APPROVAL_UUID` and `npm run live:transfer -- reconcile APPROVAL_UUID tr_ID`. These are local operator commands; no public endpoint. Reconciliation uses the restricted read key and explicit read-only confirmation, with an exact transfer ID located in the correct payer's Stripe Dashboard/Workbench. It verifies the payer account, LIVE mode, transfer ID, exact destination, currency, amount, transfer group and approval UUID metadata. It appends a transfer observation and tracks the greatest verified reversed amount, refusing conflicting transfer IDs. A stale observation cannot erase an observed reversal. No Stripe write is made during reconciliation. No-result/unknown outcomes remain unresolved, not evidence of failure. A transfer observation confirms a transfer record, not a bank payout. `bankPayoutVerified` remains false; the separate payout ledger reports Stripe status, not independent bank receipt.
